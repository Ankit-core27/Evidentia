# STATEMENT OF SANTOSH JADHAV (AUTO-RICKSHAW DRIVER)

**Recorded at:** Sahyadri Hospital, Kothrud, Pune (bedside, ward 2)
**Date and time recorded:** 15 August 2026, 09:40 hrs
**Recorded by:** PSI Anil Pawar. Dr Kavita Rao confirmed the patient was fit to give a statement.
**Language:** Marathi, translated into English by Constable Savita More

My name is Santosh Jadhav. I am 52 years old and I live in Bhusari Colony, Kothrud. I have driven my own CNG auto-rickshaw, MH12 QR 2208, for 17 years and I have a valid badge and licence.

On the night of 14 August at about 22:10 I picked up one lady passenger, Meera madam, near the Mayur Colony bus stop. She asked me to go to Bhusari Colony. I took Lane 7 going **south** towards Karve Road. She was sitting in the back seat and she had her handbag with her.

When I reached Sahyadri Chowk the signal for me was **red**. I slowed down and I was moving very slowly, about 15 to 20 kilometres per hour, towards the line, as the signal was about to change. I was not stopping completely because I saw the cross-traffic signal had finished. When my light turned **green** I went forward. I had crossed the stop line when I saw a car coming very fast from my right, from the Deccan side, along Karve Road. I could hear the engine. I did not hear the horn. I tried to turn the handle to the left but the car hit my auto from the right side. The auto was lifted and fell on its left side. I fell with my left leg under the auto and I felt severe pain. I do not remember what happened to the passenger. I saw her lying on the road when I got out.

The car was **white**, I am certain, a Honda City. The man who got out of the car was alone. A woman did not come with him. I do not know of any woman who got out of that car.

I was **not on my phone**. I do not use my phone while driving. I had not drunk any alcohol. I do not drink on duty.

I want to complain against the car driver. He was driving very fast and he jumped the red light.
