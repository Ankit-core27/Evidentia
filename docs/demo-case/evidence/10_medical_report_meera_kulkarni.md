## 1. General condition on arrival

Conscious, oriented. Pulse 112 per minute, blood pressure 108/70 mmHg, respiratory rate 24 per minute, oxygen saturation 94 percent on room air. Glasgow Coma Scale 14/15 on arrival (confusion, eye opening and motor response normal). She vomited once in the emergency room. No loss of consciousness was reported by the patient; the bystanders at the scene could not say.

## 2. Injuries found

1. **Head:** A 5 cm lacerated wound over the **forehead**, on the right side, with underlying tenderness. CT head shows a **linear, undisplaced fracture of the frontal bone** on the right side. No intracranial bleed seen. Brain parenchyma appears normal on the scan.
2. **Left shoulder:** Swelling, bruising and tenderness over the **left clavicle**. X-ray shows a **displaced fracture of the middle third (midshaft) of the left clavicle**. The patient cannot lift her left arm.
3. **Chest:** Tenderness over the right side of the chest. X-ray shows **fractures of the right 4th, 5th and 6th ribs** (posterolateral), with a small **right-sided pulmonary contusion** in the right lung. No pneumothorax. Heart sounds normal.
4. **Left forearm:** An abrasion of 6 cm by 2 cm on the outer side of the left forearm. No fracture seen.
5. **Abdomen:** Soft, non-tender. Ultrasound (FAST) shows no free fluid. The liver, spleen and kidneys appear normal on ultrasound.
6. **Spine and pelvis:** No tenderness; X-rays normal.
7. **Right knee:** Abrasion of 3 cm by 3 cm.

## 3. Treatment given

Wound sutured (8 stitches) and dressed. Left arm in a sling for the clavicle fracture, to be reviewed by orthopaedics. Oxygen by mask for 6 hours. Admitted under the surgical team for observation.

## 4. Opinion

The injuries are consistent with a person being thrown from a vehicle in a road traffic accident. The fractures of the frontal bone, left clavicle and right ribs are **grievous** in nature (fracture of bone). The patient is out of danger at the time of reporting but remains under observation for the lung contusion for 48 hours.
