## 4. Analyst's observations

1. The sedan entered the junction **4.7 seconds after its signal turned red** and 1.7 seconds after the north-south signal turned green.
2. The sedan travelled at roughly **65 km/h** in a **40 km/h** zone.
3. The auto-rickshaw crossed the stop line on green, 1.1 seconds after its green began, at low speed.
4. The sedan crossed the double white line to overtake the scooter before the junction. This is consistent with Ms Bhosale's statement.
5. Footage shows **two persons** leaving the sedan: the driver (22:13:58) and a person in a blue garment from the front passenger door (22:14:02). The identity of the second person is not established.
6. The footage does not show the inside of the sedan. Whether the driver was holding a phone before the impact cannot be determined from the footage.
