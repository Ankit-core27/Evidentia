# STATEMENT OF RAJESH MENON (CAR DRIVER)

**Recorded at:** Kothrud Police Station, Pune
**Date and time recorded:** 15 August 2026, 11:20 hrs
**Recorded by:** PSI Anil Pawar
**Language:** Marathi/English, written down in English

I, Rajesh Menon, aged 41 years, a senior manager at Menon Analytics Pvt. Ltd., residing at Flat 402, Sai Heights, Kothrud, Pune, state as follows.

On the night of 14 August 2026 I was driving home from my office in Baner in my company car, a **silver-grey** Honda City, registration MH12 KE 4417. I was **alone in the car**. I left the office at about 21:55 and took Karve Road towards Kothrud.

I was driving at about **40 kilometres per hour**, which is the limit on that road. I always keep to the limit because the road is busy with scooters. I was in the left lane going east. Nobody was in front of me at the junction.

As I came near Sahyadri Chowk the signal was **amber** when I was still some distance away. I did not think I could stop safely, so I continued. I am sure the light was amber and had not turned red when I went over the line. I do not remember any other vehicle coming from the side road. Suddenly an auto-rickshaw appeared from my left. I think it was not slowing down and I believe the driver was in a hurry. I applied the brakes with all my strength but I could not avoid the collision. My front right side hit the auto.

At the time of the accident I was **not using my mobile phone**. I may have received a call just before, but my phone was connected to the car's Bluetooth and I was not holding it. I do not remember how long the call lasted.

I have not consumed any alcohol that evening. I had a glass of wine at a client dinner at about 20:00 hrs but nothing after that. I agreed to give a breath sample.

After the accident I got out of the car and went to the auto-rickshaw. I helped the driver. I did not run away. I called for help. I stayed at the spot until the police arrived. I did not speak to any woman who was in my car, because as I said I was alone.

I request the police to check the signal log, as I am sure it was amber for me. I am willing to cooperate with the investigation.

The above statement was read over to me in English and I confirm that it is true.

Signature: Rajesh Menon
