# STATEMENT OF PRADEEP SHINDE (INDEPENDENT EYEWITNESS AND INFORMANT)

**Recorded at:** Shinde Tea Stall, Sahyadri Chowk
**Date and time recorded:** 14 August 2026, 23:05 hrs
**Recorded by:** PSI Anil Pawar
**Language:** Marathi, translated into English

My name is Pradeep Shinde, age 47. I run Shinde Tea Stall at the north-west corner of Sahyadri Chowk. I have been here for 12 years and I know this signal well. I keep the stall open until about 23:30 every night.

Tonight at about **ten fifteen** (I am not looking at the clock, it was about 10:15) I was pouring tea for a customer when I heard a very loud screeching of tyres and then a crash. I looked up. The signal for Karve Road, the east-west road, had turned **red** some seconds before. I remember that the green arrow for the side road had just come on because the cars on Lane 7 had started to move. The **white Honda City** was coming from the Deccan side, very fast, much faster than the other cars on that road. I think it was going **sixty or seventy**, I cannot say exactly. It did not slow down until the last moment. The auto-rickshaw was coming from the Lane 7 side, from the north, and it was going slowly, like any auto in the city.

The car hit the auto on its side and the auto flipped over. There was glass everywhere. A woman, the passenger of the auto, was thrown out onto the road. She was bleeding.

The car stopped a little further ahead. The driver got out. He looked very shaken. He was on his phone, I think, because he was holding it to his ear and talking while he walked. I am not sure if he was calling someone or someone had called him.

I also saw **a young woman** get out of the front passenger seat of the white car. She was wearing a blue kurta. She stood there for a short while, speaking to the driver. Then she went away on foot towards the Paud Road side. A few minutes later she got into a passing auto-rickshaw and left. This was before the police van reached the spot. I did not see her again. I can say I saw this clearly because the street lamp is right there.

I called **112** from my mobile at once and I gave the details. I also called the ambulance number. People from the pharmacy came to help the injured woman.

I did not know any of these people before tonight.

Signature: Pradeep Shinde
