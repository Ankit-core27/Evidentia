# STATEMENT OF NEHA BHOSALE (SCOOTER RIDER, WITNESS)

**Recorded at:** Kothrud Police Station
**Date and time recorded:** 15 August 2026, 14:00 hrs
**Recorded by:** PSI Anil Pawar

I am Neha Bhosale, 29 years old, working as a physiotherapist at a clinic on Karve Road. I ride a Honda Activa, MH12 ZB 9031.

On 14 August at around 22:13 I was riding home along Karve Road, going **east** towards Kothrud. I was in the left lane at about 30 kilometres per hour. About **150 metres before Sahyadri Chowk** a car came up behind me and overtook me on the right, crossing the double line in the middle of the road. It was a **light-coloured sedan, white or grey**, it was hard to say under the street lights. It passed me at a very high speed, much faster than I was going. I would say at least **60 kilometres per hour**. My scooter wobbled and I was scared. I saw the car's rear lights going away from me. I also remember seeing the driver's face lit up by his screen. I think he had a phone or the car's screen lit in front of him, but I cannot be sure.

I saw the traffic signal ahead. It was **red for Karve Road**. I started to slow down for the red. I saw the car speed towards the junction without slowing. Then I saw brake lights flash briefly and heard the screech and the crash. I stopped my scooter about 30 metres short of the line. I got off and saw the auto-rickshaw on its side. I rushed to help the passenger lady. I was the one who gave her my dupatta to press on her forehead.

I did not see how many people were in the car. I only saw the driver after the crash. A girl may have been with him, I am not sure, there was a girl in a blue dress near the car but I do not know if she came from the car.

I am ready to be a witness.

Signature: Neha Bhosale
