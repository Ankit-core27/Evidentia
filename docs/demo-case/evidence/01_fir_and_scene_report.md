### 1. Information received

At 22:14 hrs on 14 August 2026 a 112 emergency call was logged by the Pune control room reporting a collision between a car and an auto-rickshaw at Sahyadri Chowk. PCR Van Kothrud-3 was dispatched and reached the spot at 22:21 hrs. PSI Anil Pawar took over the scene at 22:34 hrs.

Note 1: The time of "about 22:15 hrs" is the time the informant estimated when he gave his statement. The signal controller log and the junction CCTV (see separate exhibits) fix the collision itself at 22:13:49 hrs. The FIR time is therefore the time the matter was reported, not the time of impact.

### 2. Vehicles involved

1. **Vehicle A:** White Honda City sedan, registration **MH12 KE 4417**, registered to Menon Analytics Pvt. Ltd., Baner, Pune. Driven by **Rajesh Menon**, 41, of Flat 402, Sai Heights, Kothrud. The vehicle was travelling **eastbound** on Karve Road, from the Deccan side towards the Paud Road side.
2. **Vehicle B:** CNG auto-rickshaw (three-wheeler), registration **MH12 QR 2208**, black and yellow, owner-driver **Santosh Jadhav**, 52, of Bhusari Colony, Kothrud. It was travelling **southbound** on Lane 7 with one passenger, **Meera Kulkarni**, 34, a school teacher of Mayur Colony.
3. **Vehicle C (not involved in the collision):** Honda Activa scooter, registration **MH12 ZB 9031**, ridden by **Neha Bhosale**, 29. Ms Bhosale was behind Vehicle A on Karve Road and stopped after the collision.

### 3. Road and junction description

- Karve Road runs east-west. It has one lane each way, each 3.5 m wide, separated by a painted double white centre line. Traffic keeps to the **left**.
- Lane 7 meets Karve Road at right angles from the north and from the south. It has one lane each way, each 3.5 m wide.
- The junction is controlled by a four-phase traffic signal (controller ID J-17-09). Stop lines are painted about 8 m from the centre of the junction on all four arms.
- The posted speed limit on Karve Road is **40 km/h**. The posted limit on Lane 7 is 30 km/h.
- Road surface: dry asphalt, no potholes. Street lighting (sodium lamps) working on all four corners. Weather: clear, 27 degrees C.
- A bus shelter stands at the north-west corner. Shinde Tea Stall stands beside it. A pharmacy occupies the south-east corner.

### 5. Scene findings

1. The sedan's skid marks run in a straight line along the **eastbound lane** and end at the point of impact. No skid marks were found from the auto-rickshaw.
2. The sedan's damage is to the **front bumper and front right corner**. The auto-rickshaw's damage is to the **right (west) side**, which faced the sedan.
3. The sedan's airbags did not deploy. The auto-rickshaw has no airbags.
4. The auto-rickshaw was lifted and rolled onto its left side. The passenger was thrown out of the auto's open right side and came to rest on the road at the position of the handbag.
5. No brake fluid or other mechanical failure was apparent at the scene. Detailed vehicle inspection has been requested from the RTO (see MVI report).

### 6. Persons injured

1. **Meera Kulkarni** (auto passenger): conscious, bleeding from the forehead, complaining of pain in the left shoulder and right chest. Shifted by ambulance to Sahyadri Hospital, Kothrud.
2. **Santosh Jadhav** (auto driver): conscious, complaining of severe pain in the left leg. Shifted by the same ambulance.
3. **Rajesh Menon** (car driver): walking, complaining of chest discomfort from the seat belt. Examined at the same hospital and released after treatment.

### 7. Action taken

- Both vehicles seized under Section 106 BNSS and stored at Kothrud Police Station yard.
- Breath analyser test conducted on Mr Menon at the hospital (result in a separate exhibit).
- Mr Menon's mobile phone seized for call detail records.
- Request sent to Pune Municipal Corporation Traffic Signal Cell for the controller log, and to the Traffic Branch for CCTV footage from KR-CAM-07 and KR-CAM-09.
- Request sent to the RTO for a Motor Vehicle Inspector's report on both vehicles.
